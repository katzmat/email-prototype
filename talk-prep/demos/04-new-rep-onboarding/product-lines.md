# Hartland Industrial — Product Lines & Knowledge Guide
*For new rep onboarding. What you need to know to walk into any customer and sound credible.*

## Our Core Lines

### 1. Bearings (45% of revenue)
**Our bread and butter.** Every machine that rotates needs bearings. This is where most customer relationships start.

**Brands we carry:**
- **SKF** — Premium. Swedish. Best-in-class for precision and reliability. Higher price point but longer life. Use for aerospace, food processing, any application where failure is expensive. *Our strongest line — we're the #1 SKF distributor in Kansas.*
- **Timken** — American-made, known for tapered roller bearings. Strong in heavy industry, mining, ag. Customers who "buy American" gravitate here. Great warranty program.
- **NTN** — Japanese quality, competitive pricing. Good alternative when customer needs premium quality but SKF pricing is too high. Strong in automotive and general industrial.
- **Dodge (ABB)** — Mounted bearings and housed units. The orange housings are everywhere in manufacturing. Easy to sell — maintenance guys know and trust Dodge. Being acquired by ABB hasn't changed product quality.
- **SealMaster** — Budget-friendly mounted units. When Dodge is overkill or the customer is price-sensitive. New "Gold Line" launching April 1 — extended warranty, improved sealing.

**What to know:**
- Bearing selection is NOT just size. Application matters — speed, load, temperature, contamination, lubrication. Get the application details before recommending. When in doubt, call SKF's application engineering line (we have a direct number).
- **The Amazon threat is real** for commodity bearings (6205, 6305, standard pillow blocks). Don't try to compete on price for $15 bearings. Compete on *expertise, availability, and the stuff Amazon can't do* — application engineering, emergency delivery, warranty support.
- Margins: 22-35% depending on brand and application. Precision/aerospace bearings are highest margin. Commodity bearings are lowest.

### 2. Power Transmission (25% of revenue)
**Everything that transfers power from a motor to a machine.** Belts, chains, gearboxes, couplings, sprockets.

**Brands we carry:**
- **Rexnord** — Couplings (grid, gear, disc) and conveyor components. Strong in food & beverage and heavy industry. Our go-to coupling line. *Watch out: Rexnord lead times have been stretched to 6-8 weeks on many items since their plant consolidation.*
- **Gates** — Belts and hose. Everyone knows Gates. Reliable, well-priced, great availability. Synchronous belt drives are a growing segment (replacing chain in many applications).
- **Dodge (ABB)** — Gear reducers and mounted bearings (yes, they cross over). Their Quantis reducer line is excellent for conveyor drives.
- **Martin Sprocket** — Sprockets, gears, conveyor pulleys. Texas-based, fast delivery. Good for custom sprocket orders — 2-week turnaround on specials.
- **Regal Beloit** — Motors and gear drives. Breadth of line is their strength. Not always the first choice in any single category but covers a lot of ground.

**What to know:**
- Power transmission is where our **application knowledge** wins deals. Anyone can sell a belt. We can tell them which belt, which tensioning system, and which drive ratio to solve their problem.
- Margins: 25-38%. Custom/engineered solutions at the top. Standard V-belts at the bottom.
- **Couplings are a gateway product.** When a coupling fails, the customer needs the bearing behind it, the motor driving it, and maybe a new shaft seal. One coupling sale leads to a full BOM.

### 3. Seals & Gaskets (12% of revenue)
**Keeps stuff in and keeps stuff out.** Shaft seals, O-rings, gaskets, mechanical seals.

**Brands we carry:**
- **Garlock** — Mechanical seals and gaskets. The gold standard for pump seals in food processing and chemical applications. Premium pricing, worth it.
- **John Crane** — Mechanical seals for heavy-duty pump applications. Competes with Garlock at the top end.
- **SKF (Chicago Rawhide/CR)** — Oil seals. The default for shaft seals across all industries. Wide catalog, good availability.
- **Parker Hannifin** — O-rings and hydraulic seals. (Note: We carry Parker seals but not their hose/fittings — yet. That's the fluid power expansion opportunity Jim is evaluating.)

**What to know:**
- Seals are consumable — they wear out and need regular replacement. This means **repeat business**. Get a customer set up with the right seal program and they order monthly.
- Food-grade and FDA-compliant seals are a specialty of ours. Many competitors don't stock these. Emphasize this with food processing customers.
- Margins: 30-40%. Highest margin line we carry. Seals are often sold as part of a repair kit, which adds value.

### 4. Motors & Drives (10% of revenue)
**Electric motors and the electronics that control them.**

**Brands we carry:**
- **WEG** — Brazilian motor manufacturer, excellent quality-to-price ratio. Our primary motor line. Strong in general-purpose and severe-duty applications.
- **Baldor (ABB)** — Premium motors. The name in American-made motors. Spec'd by many engineers. Higher cost but bulletproof. ABB's acquisition hasn't hurt the brand.
- **Regal Beloit (Marathon/Leeson)** — Broad line, good value. Fractional HP motors through large industrial.
- **ABB Drives** — Variable frequency drives (VFDs). Energy savings sell these — a VFD on a pump motor can save 30-50% on electricity. Utilities sometimes offer rebates.

**What to know:**
- Motors are increasingly sold WITH drives. If a customer is buying a motor, ask about their control needs. VFD + motor combos are higher margin and stickier.
- **Energy efficiency sells.** IE3/IE4 premium efficiency motors have a higher upfront cost but the energy savings payback in 12-18 months. Help customers do the math.
- We stock common NEMA frame sizes. Specials are 4-8 weeks. If a customer's motor dies and they need a replacement TODAY, check stock first — emergency motor sales are our highest-margin transactions.
- Margins: 20-30%. Higher on drives and special-purpose motors.

### 5. Valves (3% of revenue — GROWING)
**Newest product line. Big growth opportunity.**

**Brands we carry:**
- **Bray International** — Butterfly valves and actuators. Excellent for HVAC and process applications. Our primary valve line.
- **Apollo Valves** — Ball valves. Broad catalog, good availability, competitive pricing.
- **Nibco** — Gate, globe, and check valves. Primarily for plumbing/mechanical contractor applications.

**What to know:**
- We're new to valves. Our valve inventory is thin — most orders are drop-shipped from manufacturers. This creates lead time problems (see: Midwest Mechanical situation).
- The Wichita Valve & Fitting acquisition by a national chain has created an opening — local contractors are unhappy with the new ownership's service. We can pick up business if we can solve the inventory/lead time gap.
- **Don't oversell our valve capabilities** until we build inventory and expertise. Better to under-promise and over-deliver than to win a deal and botch the fulfillment.
- Margins: 25-35%. Actuated valve packages are highest margin.

### 6. Linear Motion (3% of revenue)
**Rails, ball screws, actuators — precision stuff for machine builders and automation.**

**Brands we carry:**
- **THK** — Japanese. The premium linear motion brand. Aerospace and precision machine applications.
- **Hiwin** — Taiwanese. Good quality, 30-40% less than THK. For applications where THK is overkill.

**What to know:**
- This is a technical sell. You need to understand load ratings, accuracy classes, and preload. Let the customer's engineers drive the spec, then make sure we can supply it.
- Small revenue today but growing fast as manufacturing automates. Jensen Manufacturing and Novacoil are our biggest linear motion accounts.
- Margins: 28-35%.

### 7. MRO Supplies (2% of revenue)
**Maintenance, repair, and operations consumables. Lubricants, adhesives, safety supplies, hand tools.**

**What to know:**
- Low margin, high volume. We carry this because customers want one-stop shopping. If we don't have their MRO basics, they go to Grainger or Fastenal and might take their bearing business with them.
- Don't lead with MRO — lead with our specialty lines and let MRO follow.
- This is the category most vulnerable to Amazon Business.

## Common Cross-Sell Opportunities

| If they're buying... | Also recommend... | Why |
|---------------------|-------------------|-----|
| Bearings | Seals, lubrication | Bearings fail when seals leak or lubrication fails |
| Couplings | Bearings, alignment tools | Coupling failure often caused by misalignment, which kills bearings |
| Motors | VFDs, belts/couplings | Complete drive system is more valuable than a motor alone |
| Conveyor bearings | Belts, idlers, pulleys | Conveyor maintenance is a system, not individual components |
| Pump bearings | Mechanical seals, couplings | Pump rebuild = bearings + seals + coupling every time |

## Pricing Philosophy
- We are NOT the cheapest. Don't try to be. We compete on **expertise, availability, and service**.
- Our value proposition: "We're the people you call at 2 AM when your line is down, and we show up."
- Standard discount authority: up to 10% for reps, 10-15% needs sales manager (Jeff) approval, over 15% needs VP approval.
- **Never discount without getting something in return.** "I can do 8% if you commit to the full bearing package, not just the line-down items."
